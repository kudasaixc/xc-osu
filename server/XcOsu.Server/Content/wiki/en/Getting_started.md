# Getting started

xc-osu! is the lazer client running in your browser. This page takes you from opening the site to your first play.

## What you need

- A computer with a graphics card or integrated graphics from the last several years.
- A recent browser. Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera are the best tested. Firefox works. Safari and phones are experimental.
- **Hardware acceleration turned on** in the browser. Without it the game is drawn by the processor and is too slow to play. The launcher tells you when this is the case; see [Performance](/wiki/Performance).
- About 1 GB of free disk space for the browser's cache and your beatmaps.

## First launch

The first visit downloads the engine, which is large. Leave the tab open until it finishes. Later visits start from the browser's cache.

While it downloads you see the launcher: news, the state of the port, and options. You do not need to change any option to start.

## Create an account

You can play without an account, but scores are only saved online with one. On the game's main menu, open the account panel at the top right and choose to register. Accounts are specific to xc-osu!: an account from the official game does not work here, and nothing you do here is sent there.

## Get beatmaps

Open the beatmap listing from the main menu, search, and click a beatmap to download it. You can also drop an `.osz` file onto the page. More on the [Beatmaps](/wiki/Beatmaps) page.

## Where your data is

Beatmaps, skins and settings are stored by your browser, on your computer, for this site only. If you clear the site's data, use a private window, or switch browser, they are not there. Your account, scores and rank are on the server and follow you everywhere.
