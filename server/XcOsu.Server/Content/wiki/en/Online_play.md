# Online play

Everything online in xc-osu! runs on xc-osu!'s own servers. Scores, rankings and accounts here are separate from the official game.

## Scores and pp

A score is sent when you finish a beatmap while signed in. The server recomputes its performance points with the game's own calculators. Your total pp is a weighted sum of your best scores: the best counts fully, each following one a little less.

## Rankings

There are rankings by performance, by ranked score and by country. Only plays made on xc-osu! count. They are visible in the game and on the [website](https://osu.xcsoftworks.com/web/rankings).

## Medals

Medals unlock for reaching milestones, such as passing or full-comboing beatmaps of a given star rating or holding a long combo. The game notifies you when you earn one, and your profile lists them.

## Daily challenge

One beatmap per day, the same for everybody. Playing it on consecutive days builds a streak, and your profile records how often you placed in the top 10 % and top 50 %.

## Multiplayer

- **Lobbies**: create a room or join one from the multiplayer screen.
- **Playlists**: rooms that stay open, where everyone plays the same list in their own time.
- **Quick play** and **ranked play**: join a queue and get matched with other players. Ranked play keeps a rating that moves with your results.

## Spectating

You can watch a friend play live from the dashboard or from their profile. Replays of scores can be watched from leaderboards.

## Teams

A team has a name, a short tag shown next to its members' names, and a flag. Create or join one from your [account settings](https://osu.xcsoftworks.com/web/home/account/edit). A player belongs to one team at a time.

## Chat

Public channels, private messages, and a channel for each multiplayer room.
