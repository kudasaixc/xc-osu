# Your account

## Creating one

Register from the game's account panel, or on the [website](https://osu.xcsoftworks.com/web/register). You need a username, an e-mail address and a password. The same account works in the game and on the site.

## Editing your profile

Open [account settings](https://osu.xcsoftworks.com/web/home/account/edit) on the website. You can change:

- your avatar and cover image (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP);
- your country, location, interests, occupation and links;
- your play style (mouse, keyboard, tablet, touch);
- the text of your profile page, with simple formatting: headings, bold, italics, lists, links, images, tables;
- your default game mode;
- your password.

## Signing in and out

Signing in on the website and in the game are independent. Signing out of one leaves the other signed in.

## Forgotten password

There is no automatic password reset by e-mail yet. See [Reporting a problem](/wiki/Reporting_a_problem) for how to reach us.

## Friends

Add a friend from their profile, in the game or on the site. Your friends list is shared between both.
