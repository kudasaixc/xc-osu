# Troubleshooting

## The game is extremely slow (under 30 frames per second)

Almost always: the browser is not using your graphics card. Follow the first section of [Performance](/wiki/Performance).

## The page stays on the loading screen

- The first download is large. Give it time on a slow connection, and keep the tab in front.
- Reload once. A download interrupted halfway is resumed from the cache.
- Make sure the browser is up to date. The game needs WebGL2 and shared memory between threads, which old browsers lack.
- Private windows have small storage limits and may fail. Use a normal window.

## "Out of memory", or the tab closes by itself

The game needs a lot of memory. Close other tabs and applications. On a phone or tablet, use the iPhone / iPad preset in the launcher; some devices still cannot hold the game.

## My beatmaps and settings disappeared

They are stored by the browser for this site. They are lost if the site's data was cleared, if you are in a private window, or if you changed browser or profile. Your account and scores are not affected.

## No sound

Click once inside the game: browsers block sound until the page has been interacted with. More on the [Sound](/wiki/Sound) page.

## A key is stuck, or scrolling changes the volume

A modifier key was held when the window lost focus. Press and release `Alt`, `Ctrl` and `Shift` once.

## Escape leaves fullscreen instead of pausing

In Firefox and Safari this cannot be prevented. In Chromium browsers, a short press reaches the game and a long press leaves fullscreen.

## Downloads of beatmaps fail

The beatmap mirrors are sometimes unavailable. Try again a minute later.

## I cannot sign in

Accounts are specific to xc-osu!. An account from the official game does not exist here: register a new one.

## Still stuck

See [Reporting a problem](/wiki/Reporting_a_problem).
