# Reporting a problem

A good report gets a problem fixed. A report that says "it lags" usually cannot be acted on.

## Before you report

- Look at [Troubleshooting](/wiki/Troubleshooting). The most common problems have a known cause.
- Reload the page once and check whether it happens again.

## What to include

1. **What you were doing**, step by step, and what happened instead of what you expected.
2. **Your browser and its version**, and your operating system.
3. **Your graphics card.** The launcher shows it in its system information.
4. **The engine's console.** Press `Ctrl+Alt+L` in game, and copy the last lines, especially anything in red.
5. For performance problems, a capture of the graph shown by `Ctrl+Alt+P`.

If diagnostics are enabled in the launcher, crashes and stutters are already recorded with the technical details. Saying roughly when it happened and what your username is lets us find the matching record.

## Where to send it

Send your report to kudasaixc, the developer of xc-osu!, with a private message in the game's chat, or in the game's public chat channel where other players can confirm the problem.

## What to expect

xc-osu! is developed by one person. Reports are read, and crashes and anything that loses data come first.
