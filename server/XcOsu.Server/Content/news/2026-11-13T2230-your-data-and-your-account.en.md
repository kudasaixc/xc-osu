# Your data, your account

## Download everything

Settings now has a **Your data** section. *Download my data* gives you a JSON file with everything the server keeps about your account: your profile, your scores, favourites, friends and blocks, comments and votes, chat messages, notifications, team membership, achievements, rank history, reports you sent and when your sessions expire. Your password is not in it, not even as a hash.

## Delete your account

In the same section, *Delete my account* erases the account for good once you enter your password and type your username. Everything goes in one transaction: scores and their replays, screenshots, avatar and cover, friends lists in both directions, private conversations and the messages you sent. You are signed out everywhere at once.

Two things stay, on purpose. A comment that other players replied to is kept as an empty "deleted" entry so their replies still make sense. And a team you led passes to its longest-standing member instead of disappearing; it is disbanded only if you were alone in it.

## Backups

The database and the uploaded files (avatars, covers, replays, screenshots) are now backed up every night on the server, and the last seven days are kept. A deleted account therefore disappears from the backups within a week too.

## Privacy and legal pages

The [Privacy](/wiki/Privacy) page now says how long each thing is kept, why (running the service, or fixing crashes for diagnostics), what the web server logs record and when they go (14 days), and how to use your rights, including a complaint to your data protection authority. A new [Legal](/wiki/Legal) page sets out that xc-osu! is a non-commercial fan project, not affiliated with ppy, and under which licences the game code and resources are used.
