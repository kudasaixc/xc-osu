# The website opens

Until today, clicking "view in browser" on a profile or a beatmap led nowhere. xc-osu! now has its website, at [osu.xcsoftworks.com/web](https://osu.xcsoftworks.com/web/).

## What is on it

- **Profiles**, with scores, medals, recent activity and the rank chart. Yours has an edit button.
- **Account settings**: avatar, cover, country, the text of your profile page, play style, password, and your team.
- **Rankings** by performance, by score and by country.
- **Beatmaps**: search, difficulty details, leaderboards, comments, and a direct download.
- **Teams**, **news**, the **changelog** and a **wiki**.

You sign in with the same account as in the game. Signing in on the site does not sign you out of the game, and the reverse is true as well.

## Links from the game now arrive somewhere

Every link the game opens in a browser leads to a page of this site: a player's profile, a beatmap, a score, a multiplayer room, a news post. That includes the links the game was built with for the official website. "Report an issue", for example, now opens xc-osu!'s own page explaining how to report a problem.

## A wiki and a changelog, in the game as well

The wiki and changelog panels inside the game used to be empty. They are now filled by xc-osu!, in English and in French, and the same pages are readable on the site. The wiki starts small: getting started, performance, sound, controls, online play, troubleshooting. It will grow with the questions that come up most.

## A warning when the browser is not using your graphics card

This one came from a player whose game ran at 20 frames per second on a recent graphics card. The cause was the browser: hardware acceleration was off, so every frame was drawn by the processor. The launcher now detects this and says so, with the steps to fix it in your browser. If you see that message, follow it before changing anything else.
