# The online side is complete

When xc-osu! opened, an account gave you scores, pp and a leaderboard. One day later the rest of what players expect from an online rhythm game is there too.

## New on the server

- **Medals.** They unlock as you play and the game tells you on the spot.
- **Daily challenge.** One beatmap a day for everyone, with streaks and a note of whether you finished in the top 10 % or top 50 %.
- **Quick play and ranked play.** Join the queue, get matched, play. Ranked play keeps a rating per player.
- **Teams.** Create one, pick a tag and a flag, and let others join.
- **Profiles.** Pinned scores, recent activity, a rank history that gets one point per day, your own avatar and cover.
- **Comments and votes** on beatmaps, and beatmap tags.
- **Chat rooms for multiplayer lobbies**, next to the public channels and private messages.

## Where the beatmaps come from

xc-osu! does not host beatmaps. Searching, browsing and downloading go through public beatmap mirrors, and the game falls back to another mirror when one does not answer. Search understands the usual filters: `stars>=6`, `ar>9`, `length<3m`, `status=ranked`, `creator=…`.

## How pp is calculated

The server runs the game's own difficulty and performance calculators on the beatmap you played. A play here is worth the same pp it would be worth with the same client anywhere else. Rankings, however, only count plays made on xc-osu!.

## Still being checked

Multiplayer and spectating are in place and have been driven end to end by automated players. They have had less time with real people in real browsers than solo play. If a lobby misbehaves, tell us what you were doing.
